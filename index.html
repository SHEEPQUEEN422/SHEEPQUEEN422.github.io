About

I'm an interdisciplinary analyst and product researcher working at the intersection of user behavior, data, experimentation, and product strategy. I hold an M.S. in Computational Social Science from UC San Diego (Fellowship Awarded, GPA 3.82/4.0) and an LL.B. in Sociology from Shanghai University (GPA 3.82/4.0).


My experience spans growth analytics, mixed-method user research, statistical modeling, behavioral measurement, and AI product prototyping across consumer, SaaS, and research settings. I work fluently in SQL, Python, R, and TypeScript/Next.js.


Whether analyzing multi-step funnel conversion at Think Academy, conducting competitive & user research for PropTech SaaS at Settlyfe, or building human-AI interaction experiments at Infinitus Systems, I focus on turning ambiguous behavioral evidence into concrete product and growth decisions.
