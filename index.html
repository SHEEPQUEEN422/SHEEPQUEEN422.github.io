About

I'm an analyst with an M.S. in Computational Social Science from UC San Diego (fellowship awarded) and an LL.B. in Sociology from Shanghai University. I work in SQL, Python, and R.


Most of my useful work has looked like this. At Sunway Expo, reported engagement across three separate source systems had quietly stopped reconciling with the downstream records of who actually became a qualified lead. Nobody had noticed, because each system was internally consistent with itself. I traced the discrepancy to where it started and corrected the reporting — and the corrected numbers changed where we invested. The top channel by engagement was not the top channel by lead quality.


That's the part of analysis I care about: not producing a number faster, but establishing whether the number deserves to be believed. I build that in — Reflo, a working assessment system I designed and built, screens every response for validity before it scores anything.


I'm looking for analyst and research roles where the measurement itself is part of the job: product and growth analytics, quantitative UX research, and consumer insights.
